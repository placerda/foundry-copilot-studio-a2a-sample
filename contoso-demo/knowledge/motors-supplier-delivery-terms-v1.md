## Fictional proposal

Contoso Motors is a fictional vehicle manufacturer. It requested 500 interior
trim sets by Friday. The fictional parts supplier proposes 300 sets on Friday
and 200 sets on Monday. These are proposed quantities, not verified inventory.
The total remains 500, but 200 would arrive after the requested Friday deadline.
Friday and Monday are relative labels, not confirmed calendar dates.

## Buyer acceptance conditions

The fictional original condition is complete delivery by Friday. A partial
shipment does not meet that schedule unless an authorized purchasing
representative accepts a revised schedule in writing before dispatch.
The acceptance must identify the order, both quantities, both dates and any
agreed transport-charge changes. Silence is not approval.

## Recommended next step

Send the proposed split to the authorized purchasing representative.
Explain the Friday shortfall of 200 sets and the proposed Monday balance.
Request written acceptance and confirmation of any transport-charge change.
Until that exists, describe the proposal as pending. Do not promise that
the original deadline is satisfied.

## Evidence limits

No actual order number, inventory, receiving capacity, calendar date, penalty,
compensation or named approver is supplied. The agent cannot approve a purchase,
waive a condition, reserve stock, dispatch goods or schedule transport.
Racks and returnable boxes are packaging in this demonstration, not additional
trim sets or proof that a return collection has been agreed.
