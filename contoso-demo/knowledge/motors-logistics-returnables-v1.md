# Contoso Motors returnable packaging coordination

Source ID: CONTOSO-MOTORS-LOGISTICS-001
Version: 1
Provider: Contoso Motors Logistics Agent
Classification: Synthetic demonstration data
Sharing scope: Approved CONTOSO demo callers

This fictional internal-logistics reference describes a proposed process for
transport racks and returnable boxes. It is embedded in the separate Prompt
Agent's instructions. It is not retrieved from a knowledge base, a live
warehouse system or the legacy Knowledge Hosted agent.

## Receiving coordination

The fictional order requests 500 interior trim sets by Friday. The supplier
proposes 300 on Friday and 200 on Monday. This proposal is pending purchasing
approval. It establishes neither available stock nor receiving capacity.
Before preparing a receiving plan, ask the authorized business contacts to
confirm dates, quantities, receiving capacity and unloading responsibilities.
Do not treat trim-set quantities as rack or box counts.

## Returnable packaging process

Propose recording the packaging type, counts received, counts emptied,
condition, ownership and the order reference when that reference is supplied.
Separate loaded and empty packaging in a return area whose capacity has been
confirmed. Ask the supplier and Contoso Motors to agree business
responsibilities and a collection window in writing before a pilot.
These are recommendations, not completed warehouse actions or commitments.

## Recommended next step

Request the missing rack and box counts, available return-area capacity and
the business roles responsible for collection. Ask authorized representatives
to confirm the collection arrangements. Report what remains unconfirmed.
Do not invent contacts, dimensions, quantities, dates, fees or capacity.

improvement in this source. Friday and Monday are not actual calendar dates.
The agent can explain this proposed process but cannot confirm stock, approve
a purchase, reserve space, update inventory, accept a delivery change, issue a
return authorization or schedule transport.
